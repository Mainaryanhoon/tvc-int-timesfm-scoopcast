# TimesFM V2 Demo Datasets

## Retail — `retail_sales_revenue_v2.csv`
Frostline Creamery. Use the dashboard buttons **Sales** and **Revenue**.
Six product series. Includes short/irregular promotions, discount, marketing, temperature, rainfall, footfall, festivals, competitor promotions, stock availability and stockouts.

## Automotive — `automotive_inventory_v1.csv`
Apex Motors. Use the dashboard **Inventory** button.
Ten component series. Includes component consumption, opening/closing inventory, receipts, safety stock, supplier lead time/disruptions, production volume, festivals and plant maintenance.
`automotive_bom_reference.csv` maps vehicle models to component quantities.

## OTT — `ott_viewership_v1.csv`
StreamPulse. Viewership forecasting with six content series. Includes viewers, watch hours, sessions, releases/premieres, marketing, festivals, holidays and competitor releases.

All datasets contain two years of daily synthetic data. They are deliberately structured for forecasting/backtesting demonstrations rather than being random numbers. Promotions are short and irregular rather than one continuous phase.
